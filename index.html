The Experience

A Culinary Journey Through Marrakech

From the vibrant souks to your plate, every moment of the Art Darmed Event cooking class is crafted to ignite your senses and connect you to Moroccan heritage.

01

The Souk Tour

Start your journey at the source. Walk through the bustling spice markets of the Medina, hand-picking saffron, cumin, and preserved lemons alongside our local guide.

02

The Preparation

Roll couscous by hand, master the art of the tagine, and learn family recipes passed down through generations — all inside our riad kitchen.

03

The Tasting

Savor your creations in the shade of our courtyard, paired with fresh mint tea and traditional Moroccan pastries. The perfect ending to a perfect day.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the cooking class held?

In our riad on Derb Zaouia, in the heart of the Marrakech Medina (near Rte Sidi Abdelaziz). We send you a map pin and meet you at an easy landmark to walk you in.

What does the cooking class include?

A guided walk to the souk to choose fresh ingredients, hands-on cooking of a full Moroccan meal — starter, main and dessert — and dining together on our rooftop terrace with mint tea. We're happy to adapt for vegetarian and gluten-free guests.

How much does a cooking class cost?

Our Home Made menu is 350 MAD and the festive Ceremonial menu is 700 MAD. Booking and payment are arranged simply over WhatsApp.

Do I need to book in advance?

Yes — classes stay small (max 8 guests) and personal, so we recommend booking a day or two ahead on WhatsApp. We usually confirm within 2 hours.

Can I also stay overnight in the riad?

Yes. We have rooms in our restored riad in the Marrakech Medina from 600 MAD per night, so you can pair a stay with your cooking experience.

Our Story

Welcome to Art Darmed Event

In the heart of the ancient Medina, behind a hand-carved cedarwood door, lies Art Darmed Event — a traditional riad that has been our family home for generations. Every tile, every arch, every courtyard fountain carries the memory of countless meals shared and stories told.

We opened our kitchen to the world because we believe food is the most honest expression of culture. When you cook with us, you're not just learning recipes — you're becoming part of a living tradition that stretches back centuries.
