About This Room

Wake up steps from the rooftop terrace. Warm Moroccan textiles and carved plasterwork details make this room a perfect blend of tradition and comfort. La Chambre de l'Atlas is named for its unique position just below the rooftop, where you can enjoy sunrise views over the Medina and the Atlas Mountains.

The room is adorned with embroidered pillows, handwoven bedspreads, and intricate stucco carvings that reflect centuries of Moroccan artistry. Step through the door and you're moments from the panoramic rooftop terrace with views over the Atlas Mountains.
